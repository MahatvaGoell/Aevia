What's inside

A planner that knows what's due

Tell Aevia about your semester once. It keeps track of the rest.

Weekly timetable

Built for you from your exams and deadlines. Subjects are mixed each day, and the day before every exam is kept for revision.

Today's playlist

Today's sessions play like tracks. Start the focus timer, and the session checks itself off when the time is up.

Subjects and % ready

Each subject shows a countdown to its exam and how ready you are, based on what you've finished and how confident you feel.

Deadlines

Assignments with priority and status: to do, in progress, done or overdue. Lab reports get finished a day early.

Tips and alerts

A daily tip based on your plan, plus a warning when you're short on prep time or studying too many hours in a row.

Replan by chat

Type “I'm sick on Thursday” and your week is rebuilt around it. Aevia moves the sessions, not your exam dates.

How it works

From a paragraph to a full plan

Step 1

Describe your semester

Write it the way you'd tell a friend: “DBMS exam on the 7th, OS lab due this Friday, I'm a night owl.” Aevia fills in your dashboard.

Step 2

Get a timetable

The planner builds your week, putting the most urgent work first and weighting each subject by priority, difficulty and confidence.

Step 3

Adjust as you go

Mark sessions done, teach topics back, or tell it about a bad day. Your plan updates and shows you exactly what moved.

Is the AI just making up a schedule?

No. A scheduling engine builds the timetable, so every plan fits your hours and meets every deadline. The AI reads what you write, explains the plan and changes its inputs.

If the AI is ever unavailable, your planner keeps working.
